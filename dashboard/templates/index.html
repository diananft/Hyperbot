Quick Guide

How It Works

Hyperbot analyzes 5 assets across multiple timeframes using 30+ technical indicators, orderbook data, and pattern recognition. When enough signals align (score > 0.5), it enters a position automatically.

Understanding Signals

  • Score > +0.8 = Strong long signal (full size entry)
  • +0.5 to +0.8 = Moderate long (60% size)
  • -0.5 to +0.5 = Neutral (no trade)
  • -0.5 to -0.8 = Moderate short (60% size)
  • Below -0.8 = Strong short signal (full size)

Tip: If signals stay near zero for a while, the market is ranging. The bot intentionally avoids trading in choppy conditions to protect your capital.

Position Management

  • Stop loss: set at 1.5x ATR from entry
  • TP1 (40%): closes at 1.5x risk reward
  • TP2 (30%): closes at 2.5x risk reward
  • TP3 (30%): trails with ATR-based stop
  • Time exit: closes after 40 candles with small profit

Safety Protections

Max 2% equity risk per trade

Max 5x leverage cap

3% daily loss = halt until tomorrow

10% drawdown = close everything

5 losses in a row = 4 hour pause

Max 3 positions at once

Kill Switch: The red button cancels all orders and closes all positions at market price immediately. Use only in emergencies. You can also press Ctrl+C in the terminal.

Getting Started Tips

  • Start in paper mode for 48+ hours
  • When going live, use 25% size first (risk_per_trade: 0.004)
  • Check the logs/ folder for detailed signal analysis
  • The dashboard auto-refreshes every 10 seconds
  • You need funds in Perps balance (not Spot)
